Why does macOS show a warning?

Short answer: Your Mac is 100% safe. Apple just charges $99 every single year.

1. First-launch alert • 2. Click "Open Anyway" in Privacy & Security

The Real Story

When you first launch MacHinge, macOS Gatekeeper shows a prompt stating Apple could not verify the application. That’s because Apple requires an active $99/year developer membership to notarize apps. As a solo indie developer building free & open-source software in my spare time, I haven’t paid the annual fee yet!

100% Safe & Open Source

MacHinge is completely open-source under the MIT license. There is zero telemetry, zero analytics, and zero network calls. You can inspect, audit, and build every single line of code directly on GitHub.

How to open on macOS:

  1. When the warning popup appears on first launch, click Done.
  2. Open System Settings on your Mac and navigate to Privacy & Security.
  3. Scroll down to the Security section and click Open Anyway next to “MacHinge” was blocked to protect your Mac.
  4. Authenticate with your Touch ID or password to launch MacHinge.

Support open-source development

MacHinge is free and open-source. Contributions help fund the annual Apple Developer membership for out-of-the-box notarization and ongoing macOS updates.
