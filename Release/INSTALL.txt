================================================================================
                            MacHinge Installation Guide
================================================================================

HOW TO INSTALL:
1. Open MacHinge-0.1.0.dmg or expand MacHinge-0.1.0.zip.
2. Drag "MacHinge.app" into your "/Applications" folder.
3. Open "/Applications/MacHinge.app".

OPENING ON macOS (UNSIGNED BETA):
Because MacHinge is a free beta distributed directly without the Mac App Store:
1. On initial launch, macOS may indicate that the developer cannot be verified.
2. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security.
3. Scroll down to the Security section.
4. Locate the message: "MacHinge was blocked from use because it is not from an identified developer."
5. Click "Open Anyway" and authenticate with your password or Touch ID.
6. Launch MacHinge.

FIRST-TIME SETUP:
1. When MacHinge opens, follow the quick 2-step setup.
2. Grant Screen Recording permission when prompted so MacHinge can capture
   your display locally in real time (System Settings → Privacy & Security → Screen Recording).
3. Select or calibrate your preferred normal viewing angle (default: 105°).
4. Click "Get Started". MacHinge will now run quietly in your menu bar.
