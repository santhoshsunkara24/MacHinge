================================================================================
                                   MacHinge
             Physical MacBook Lid-Driven Display Folding Transition
================================================================================
Version: 0.1.0 (Build 1)
Target: Apple Silicon MacBooks (M1 / M2 / M3 / M4)
Requires: macOS 14.0 (Sonoma) or later
Distribution: Free Direct Download (Beta)

OVERVIEW:
MacHinge connects your MacBook display to its physical hinge. As you open or
close the lid, your desktop smoothly folds around the hinge axis in real time,
powered by the Apple SPU hardware lid-angle sensor, ScreenCaptureKit, and Metal.

KEY FEATURES:
• Zero-Distortion Preferred Viewing Angle:
  At your calibrated comfortable resting angle, your display is 100% normal,
  bit-exact, and sharp with zero blur, perspective, or deformation.
• Continuous Full-Range Mapping:
  Transition smoothly responds from your preferred angle all the way to closed.
• Low-Overhead Idle State:
  When stationary at normal viewing angles, rendering and capture pause to
  minimize resource usage.
• 100% On-Device Privacy:
  Screen content is processed entirely locally via Apple Metal GPU textures.
  Content is never recorded, saved, uploaded, or transmitted anywhere.

FEEDBACK & ISSUES:
MacHinge Beta is distributed directly for free.
